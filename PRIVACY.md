# ClawPhone Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 5, 2026

## Overview
ClawPhone connects you to your personal AI assistant. We respect your privacy.

## Data We Collect
- **Voice recordings**: Temporarily processed to send to your AI bot. Not stored.
- **Text messages**: Sent to your configured bot. We do not store message content.
- **Connection code**: Used only to link your app to your bot.

## Data We Don't Collect
- We do not track you
- We do not sell your data
- We do not use analytics
- We do not collect personal information

## Third Party
Messages are sent to your own configured AI bot via Telegram. We act only as a bridge.

## Your Rights
You can disconnect at any time by unlinking in Settings.

support@clawphone.app

## Changes
